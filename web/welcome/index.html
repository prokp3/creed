Privacy

Your data.Only yours.

There's no Creed company server in the middle. Your Creed is your own copy, on accounts you own, so nobody else can look at your training. Not even me.

01

Your own database

One copy, one athlete. Your data sits in a database only your account can open.

02

Locked at every table

Row-level security on every table, no public access, and keys that never reach the browser.

03

No password to steal

You sign in with Face ID. The server only ever holds the public half of your passkey.

04

Never your Garmin password

Creed reads Garmin with a key you create and can revoke. It never asks for your login.

05

Nothing handed out

Barcodes are read on your phone and looked up without saying who's asking. Your GPS routes are never copied anywhere.

06

Sealed notifications

Encrypted for your phone, so even Apple's push service can't read them.

All of it is open source, so you don't have to take my word for it.

Under the hood

How it's hosted

Every Creed is its own copy, on free accounts you own. This is the path your data takes.

  1. 01

    Garmin watch

    Records every session and night.

  2. 02

    Garmin Connect

    Read through the official Personal API, with your key.

  3. 03

    GitHub Actions

    Starts a sync every 30 minutes.

  4. 04

    Supabase

    Your own Postgres, row-level security on every table.

  5. 05

    Vercel

    Runs the API and web app, plus a daily backup sync.

  6. 06

    Your iPhone

    Home-screen app, Face ID, push notifications.
